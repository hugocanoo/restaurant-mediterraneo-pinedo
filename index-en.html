The experience

Much more than a meal

Right on the beach, Restaurant Mediterráneo has been serving traditional Valencian cooking for years: rice dishes made to order, fish and seafood, and a terrace looking out over the port of Valencia and the sea.

A place to share a paella, linger at the table and finish with a walk along Pinedo beach.
